Do I need Pilates experience to get started?

Let the studio team know you’re new when you reach out. They can talk you through the available formats and help you choose a starting point that feels comfortable.

What’s the difference between Mat and Reformer Pilates?

Mat sessions use your body and may include props. Reformer sessions use a spring-based machine to add adjustable resistance. Ask your preferred studio which class formats are currently available.

How do I choose the right location?

Browse the five listed studios above and contact the branch that works best for you. The Goa and Bengaluru teams have separate contact numbers.

Can I book if I have an injury or health concern?

Please speak with your healthcare professional about what is appropriate for you, and share relevant considerations with the studio before booking. Pilates and EMS are fitness services, not a substitute for medical advice or treatment.

How can I ask about a trial session?

Select a location in the contact form below to start a WhatsApp conversation with that studio. You can review the message before choosing whether to send it.
